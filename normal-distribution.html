X ~ N(μ, σ²)
Mean (μ)
The mean determines the centre of the distribution. Increasing μ shifts the curve to the right, while decreasing μ shifts it to the left.
Standard Deviation (σ)
The standard deviation determines the spread. A smaller σ produces a narrower and taller curve. A larger σ produces a wider and flatter curve.
Explore: Can you change the mean without changing the shape of the curve? Can you change the standard deviation without moving its centre?
